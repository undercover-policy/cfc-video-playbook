# Rules every CFC piece follows

- Content never contradicts CFC's positions on life, marriage, sexuality, parental rights, or religious liberty. If a task would, stop and ask.
- No synthetic faces or voices of real people. Staff appear only as themselves, in their own consented recordings.
- No AI-generated imagery unless the president approves it for a project, and never AI-generated people.
- Every on-screen number, date, name, quote, and verse comes from the facts file with a source; a release needs a named reviewer and approval on each.
- Music is code-composed, public domain, or CC BY with the credit on the end card and in the README. Never Eleven Music, Musicbed, Pixabay, MusicGen, or Lyria; no synthesized voices, choirs, or formant pads.
- Open-licence fonts only (Nunito Sans, EB Garamond, Oswald, Courier Prime) until the brand font licences are proven.
- No em dashes in anything a viewer sees.
- No eye bars, blurs, or 'redacted' marks on any real person. Connection lines between people or groups only for documented, sourced links.
- Captions: 2 to 4 words per chunk, each on screen at least 0.5 s, exactly the spoken words.
- Audio master: -16 LUFS integrated, -1 dBTP, true peak after AAC at or under -0.8 dBTP.
- Vertical safe zones: key content above y 1500 and clear of the right-side app buttons.
- Never weaken a check to make it pass.
- Motion blur never crosses a hard cut.
- Numbers appear at their final value: no count-ups.
- Scripture on screen uses the BSB, and a named Scripture reviewer approves every verse.
- Every lesson, decision, fact, asset, recipe, and project we learn goes into the knowledge base the same day.
- Every phone number, web address, email, and mailing address on screen matches the entity profile or a sourced fact.
- When a real person's own footage is on screen, the frame is exactly the original recording: full frame, its own captions, nothing of ours over it. Our captions, logo, panels and overlays never touch those frames.
